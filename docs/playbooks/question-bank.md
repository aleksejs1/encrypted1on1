# The 1:1 Question Bank: 60+ High-Leverage Questions

[← Back to Playbooks](README.md)

> **A comprehensive cheat sheet for managers and team leads looking to unlock deeper, more candid conversations.**

---

## How to Use This Bank

Never read questions off a checklist like an interrogator. Pick **1 or 2 targeted questions** per meeting depending on where the team member is in their lifecycle, their recent energy levels, or what they published in their anketa.

Always follow up an answer with active curiosity:
- *"Tell me more about that."*
- *"What makes you say that?"*
- *"What would the ideal outcome look like?"*
- *(5 seconds of silence to give them room to elaborate).*

---

## 1. Energy, Morale & Pulse

*Use these at the start of regular syncs or when noticing a subtle drop in motivation.*

1. **"On a scale of 1 to 5, what is your current energy level? What gave you the most energy this sprint, and what drained it?"**
   - *What to listen for:* A score below 3 indicates hidden friction, exhaustion, or personal stressors.
2. **"Which task felt like creative momentum, and which felt like a soul-draining chore?"**
   - *What to listen for:* Unbalanced distribution of chores, on-call spikes, or repetitive manual tasks.
3. **"If you could wave a magic wand and eliminate one meeting or process from your week, which one would it be?"**
   - *What to listen for:* Pointless status calls, bloated standups, or redundant rituals.
4. **"What was the most rewarding moment of your week?"**
   - *What to listen for:* What specifically sparks pride (solving an algorithm, mentoring a teammate, shipping a user-facing feature).
5. **"How does your workload feel right now: are you in the flow, treading water, or drowning?"**
   - *What to listen for:* Early warning signs of burnout before deadlines slip.
6. **"Have you had enough uninterrupted focus time (deep work) lately, or are you constantly being interrupted?"**
   - *What to listen for:* Context-switching fatigue, excessive Slack pings, fragmented calendars.

---

## 2. Process Friction, Tooling & Technical Debt

*Use these to identify organizational sand in the gears before it slows down team velocity.*

7. **"What is harder in your day-to-day work than it should be?"**
   - *What to listen for:* The classic `encrypted1on1` friction prompt: slow CI/CD pipelines, flaky tests, lack of staging data.
8. **"Where is work getting stuck between us and other teams right now?"**
   - *What to listen for:* Blocked PR reviews, bureaucratic design handoffs, delayed API specs from partner teams.
9. **"Is there any part of our codebase or architecture that you or the team are terrified to touch, and why?"**
   - *What to listen for:* Legacy landmines without test coverage, single-point-of-failure services.
10. **"What is the clunkiest internal tool or process we still tolerate just because 'that's how we've always done it'?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Outdated deployment checklists, manual permission grants.
11. **"If you had two full days to fix any technical debt in our service, what would you fix first?"**
    - *What to listen for:* High-ROI refactoring ideas that engineers usually keep to themselves.
12. **"Did you run into any permission or credential roadblocks this cycle?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Cloud IAM delays, missing database read-replicas, slow IT support.

---

## 3. Team Dynamics, Collaboration & Psychological Safety

*Use these to diagnose communication health, peer friction, and inclusion.*

13. **"How is collaboration feeling across the team right now? Does anyone feel isolated or out of the loop?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Remote disconnect, timezone silos, quiet disagreements.
14. **"How comfortable do you feel voicing a dissenting technical opinion during design reviews?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Fear of aggressive peers, dominant voices steamrolling junior feedback.
15. **"Is there anyone on the team whose contributions you think deserve more public recognition?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Invisible work (code review thoroughness, documentation, onboarding buddies).
16. **"Did you experience any awkward or tense interactions with a colleague over the past few weeks?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Simmering interpersonal conflicts before they turn toxic.
17. **"Do you feel your ideas and RFCs are taken seriously by the rest of the team?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Feelings of exclusion or seniority bias.

---

## 4. Business Context, Strategy & Alignment

*Use these to prevent engineers from becoming disconnected "ticket typers" without a sense of purpose.*

18. **"Do you feel clear on why our current sprint goals matter to our customers and the business?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Cynicism, lack of user empathy, feeling like a factory worker.
19. **"Were there any recent announcements or leadership pivots that felt confusing or contradicted our goals?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Rumors, strategic whiplash, lack of executive context.
20. **"If a candidate asked you in an interview what our company's top priority is this quarter, what would you say?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Alignment and clarity of top-level goals.
21. **"Do you feel we are building the right thing, or are we solving the wrong customer problem?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Frontline technical doubts about product viability.
22. **"What would happen if we didn't ship this current milestone for another month? Does it actually matter?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Artificial vs real urgency created by management.

---

## 5. Career Progression, Growth & Learning

*Use these during quarterly career growth dialogues or monthly development check-ins.*

23. **"Looking back at the past 6 months, which accomplishment made you genuinely proud?"**
    - *What to listen for:* What drives intrinsic motivation and professional self-worth.
24. **"What is a skill or technology you haven't had a chance to work with here that you'd love to learn?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Opportunities for stretch assignments or conference budgets.
25. **"If you imagine your role 18–24 months from now, what does your ideal week look like?"**
    - *What to listen for:* IC depth (Staff+) vs People Management vs Technical Product architecture.
26. **"What is the single biggest skill gap holding you back from reaching the next level?"**
    - *What to listen for:* System design, cross-functional communication, handling ambiguity.
27. **"Which upcoming project on our roadmap feels like a good stretch opportunity for you?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Willingness to take on calculated risks and ownership.
28. **"Do you feel you are learning faster than the market, or does your progress feel stagnant?"**
    - *What to listen for:* The #1 leading indicator of engineer attrition.

---

## 6. Upward Feedback (Managing Up)

*Use these to encourage honest feedback on your own leadership style.*

29. **"What is one thing I should start, stop, or continue doing to support you better?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Concrete adjustments to your management habits.
30. **"Where am I unintentionally creating a bottleneck for you?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Delayed PR reviews, micromanagement, slow approvals.
31. **"Did I handle any meeting or situation poorly recently where I could have shown up better?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Blind spots in your communication.
32. **"Am I giving you too much direction, too little, or just the right amount of autonomy?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Feelings of micromanagement or abandonment.
33. **"What is something you wish I understood better about your day-to-day reality?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Empathy gaps between executive planning and ground-level implementation.

---

## 7. Crisis & Burnout Diagnostics

*Use these when you notice signs of chronic exhaustion, cynicism, or emotional strain.*

34. **"I've noticed you've been working late nights and weekends recently. How are you holding up physically?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Sleep deprivation, physical symptoms of chronic stress.
35. **"If we could cancel 3 tasks off your plate right now with zero questions asked, which would they be?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Urgent relief points for task pruning.
36. **"When was the last time you took a full day off and completely disconnected from Slack and email?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Guilt about taking vacation or fear of falling behind.
37. **"Do you feel like you have to be 'always on' and respond to messages instantly?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Toxic communication expectations or fear of missing out.
38. **"How can I give you air cover so you can catch your breath this coming week?"**
    - *What to listen for:* Concrete protective actions you can take as a manager.
